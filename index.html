How it's made

Small batch is a method, not a mood.

01

Sourced simply

Plant oils, butters, herbs and botanicals — chosen because they do a job, not because they read well on a label.

02

Made in small batches

Small enough that I mix, pour and finish each one myself, and nothing sits around losing its freshness.

03

Checked by hand

Texture, scent, fill, seal — every jar is looked at before it goes out. If a batch is not right, it gets remade.

04

Labelled honestly

What is in it, what it is for, and what it will not do. If I cannot answer a question, I will tell you that too.

Straight answers

Questions I get asked most.

Will the Scalp & Growth Oil regrow my hair?
No, and I will not tell you otherwise. It is a botanical oil for scalp comfort and conditioning — a healthy scalp is a good foundation, and that is a real benefit. It is not a treatment for hair loss; if that is your concern, please see a clinician.
Is everything really made by hand?
Yes. I make the batches, fill and label the jars, and check them before they go out. That is why the range is nine products and not ninety.
I have sensitive skin. Can I use these?
Often yes — and I would rather you ask first. Nothing has artificial fragrance, and most products can be made unscented or gentler if you tell me before you order. Patch test anything new.
How do custom blends work?
You describe what your hair or skin is doing, we talk it through, and I make a small trial batch first. If it is not right, we adjust it. The whole process, step by step.
How do I order?
Call, text or email — all three reach me directly. Online ordering opens here soon; until then, that is how every order is placed.
