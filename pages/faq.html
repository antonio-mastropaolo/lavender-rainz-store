Questions, answered straight

Will the Scalp & Growth Oil regrow my hair?

No, and I will not tell you otherwise. It is a botanical oil made to keep the scalp comfortable and conditioned — a healthy scalp is a good foundation, and that is a real benefit. But it is a cosmetic product, not a treatment for hair loss. If you are losing hair and worried, please see a clinician.

Is everything really made by hand?

Yes. I make the batches, fill and label the jars, and check them before they go out. That is why the range is nine products and not ninety.

I have sensitive skin. Can I use these?

Often yes — and I would rather you ask first. Nothing has artificial fragrance, and most products can be made unscented or gentler if you tell me before you order. Patch test anything new.

How do custom blends work?

You describe what your hair or skin is doing, we talk it through, and I make a small trial batch first. Pricing depends on ingredients — ask and I will tell you before I make anything.

How long does everything last?

These are made fresh in small batches, meant to be used rather than stored for years. Keep them out of direct sun, keep water out of the jar, and I will tell you the realistic window for whatever you order.
